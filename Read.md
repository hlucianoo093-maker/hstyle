Barber Salon Booking Website – V1

Create a modern, clean, and responsive website for a Barber Salon with a simple online appointment booking system.

1. Design & Style
Modern, premium barber shop aesthetic.
Dark theme (black, charcoal, white) with subtle gold accents.
Clean typography and elegant animations.
Fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
Professional barber shop photography.
2. Homepage
Hero section with a strong headline: "Look Sharp. Feel Confident."
Short salon introduction.
Main CTA button: "Book an Appointment".
Featured services.
Meet Our Barbers section.
Customer reviews.
Contact information and opening hours.
3. Services

Display a simple list of services with prices and estimated duration.

Example:

Classic Haircut – €15 – 30 min
Skin Fade – €18 – 40 min
Beard Trim – €10 – 20 min
Haircut & Beard – €25 – 50 min
4. Booking System (Main Feature)

Create a simple 4-step booking process:

Step 1 – Choose Service

Select one available service.

Step 2 – Choose Barber

Select a barber or choose "Any Available Barber".

Step 3 – Choose Date & Time

Display a simple calendar.
Show available time slots only.
Prevent double bookings.

Step 4 – Customer Details

Full Name
Phone Number
Optional Email
Confirm Booking button.

After confirmation:

Display a booking success message.
Show appointment details.
Generate a unique booking reference.
5. Admin Dashboard

Create a simple, protected admin dashboard where the salon owner can:

View all appointments.
See bookings by date.
Confirm or cancel appointments.
Add, edit, or remove services.
Manage barbers.
Set working hours and unavailable dates.
6. Technical Requirements
Simple and reliable database for appointments.
Real booking availability, not just a visual demo.
Prevent duplicate bookings for the same barber and time.
Store customer information securely.
Basic form validation.
Clean and maintainable code.
7. Important Scope – V1

Keep the project simple. Do NOT add:

Online payments.
Customer accounts or registration.
Loyalty programs.
Complicated CRM.
AI chatbot.
Unnecessary integrations.

The main goal is to create a beautiful barber salon website where customers can easily book an appointment in less than one minute.

Focus on simplicity, speed, mobile experience, and a smooth booking process.
